Get a clearer picture before the job begins

Concrete Scanning Castle Rock is a practical step to consider when a project calls for a new opening in a slab, wall, or other concrete surface. Rebar grids, post-tension cables, and conduits may be within the work area, even when they are not visible. Locating these items before the crew starts can help the team review the proposed spot and plan its next move.

You don’t need a complete set of drawings to ask about a scan. If project documents are missing, unclear, or don’t cover the exact location, share what you do know about the intended work. The goal is to get useful information about the area where drilling, cutting, or coring is being considered.

Independent locating from Recon GPR

Recon GPR provides third-party concrete scanning and subsurface locating. We map the embedded features we’re looking for and mark clear safe-zones directly on site. Your concrete crew can then see the marked information in relation to the planned opening and consider it before proceeding.

The marks are there to inform the work plan, not to replace communication among the people involved in the job. Let the crew know that scanning has been completed, show them the marked area, and tell them if the intended opening has moved. A changed location may need to be reviewed rather than assumed to match the original scan area.

A short checklist for Castle Rock customers

Before contacting a scanning provider, take a moment to collect a few details:

  • The Castle Rock site address and how to reach the work area.
  • Where the crew expects to drill, cut, or core.
  • The approximate opening size, if it has been decided.
  • Any available plans or information about the concrete.
  • A contact who can explain changes to the work location.

One mistake to avoid is treating an old drawing as a complete picture of what is within the exact spot being changed. Another is asking a crew to proceed before someone has reviewed the scan markings. If the planned opening moves, don’t assume the information from the first location applies elsewhere. Tell your crew and Recon GPR about the change.

What does a Castle Rock concrete scan include?

Can I request scanning if I don’t have concrete plans?

Yes, you can still call and explain the planned opening and what information is available. Recon GPR can discuss the work area with you.
