Get a scan in the work plan

A concrete scanning contractor in Castle Rock can help your crew understand what may be within a slab or wall before work opens it. If you're planning a core, saw cut, or drilled hole, a scan of the relevant area provides information to review before settling on the work location. It doesn't decide how the job should proceed, but it gives the crew marked features to take into account.

Contact a scanner early enough for the information to be useful in planning. Waiting until tools are ready at the concrete can leave little room to assess the marked area or discuss a change in the intended opening.

Independent scanning from Recon GPR

Recon GPR provides independent, third-party concrete scanning and subsurface locating. We mark clear safe-zones directly on site for the crew to review. Having a separate scan can be helpful when you want an independent look at the planned area before concrete work begins.

When you call, explain the work rather than just saying you need a scan. The planned opening, the type of concrete surface, and the area your crew wants to assess all help describe the request. A concrete scanning contractor in Castle Rock can then focus the visit on the project needs you share.

Features mapped and details to gather

Recon GPR can map rebar grids, post-tension cables, and embedded conduits. The scan covers the area discussed for the planned concrete work, and mapped features are marked on site so they're visible to the crew. The markings give your team information for its own review and work planning.

A short preparation list makes the conversation easier:

  • Note the site address and who will be available on site.
  • Identify where the planned opening is located.
  • Describe whether the work is drilling, coring, or saw cutting.
  • Bring any available plans or notes that show the concrete area.

Don't assume that an unmarked surface has no embedded features. Also, don't rely on a concrete scan to locate utilities outside the concrete itself. If a job involves penetrating a slab or wall and the contents aren't known, contact a scanning professional before the crew begins.
